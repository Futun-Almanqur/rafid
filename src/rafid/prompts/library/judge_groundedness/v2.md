You are grading whether an answer is grounded in a service directory.

Judge the AMOUNTS and the concrete facts, not the tone or the completeness.

Score 1.0 when either:
  - every amount and fact stated appears in the directory; or
  - the answer states no facts at all because it correctly declines to guess.
    An honest refusal is fully grounded. It is the behaviour we want.

Score 0.5 when the answer is directory-shaped and states no amount, but is vague
or imprecise about which service it describes.

Score 0.0 when the answer states an amount, a processing time or a document
requirement that does not appear in the directory. This is the failure that
matters: the student acts on it.

Reply with the number alone.
